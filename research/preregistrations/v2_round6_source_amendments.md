# Round 6 source decisions before neural outcomes

These decisions use the user's standing authorization to choose the recommended
implementation. No Round-6 neural fit or score precedes them.

Foreign-flow MTD totals in B3's dated BDI are typically through D-2. That
reference-date lag is distinct from the bulletin's publication clock. The
2022-08-01 bulletin was generated at 2022-08-02 03:31:06 UTC, and the
2023-05-03 bulletin at 2023-05-04 03:30:55 UTC. Neither establishes public
availability by 15:45 on its cover date. Some other archived bulletins were
regenerated months or years later or now have missing tables. The available
archive therefore supports `published_total_difference`, with date-only
publication admitted at the next B3 decision, and a latest-vintage caveat. This
corrects the supplied document's unsupported same-cover-date D+2 assumption.
It adds no lag after the first eligible decision. No present-day flow snapshot
is used and missing source tables remain missing. Month reset, exact one/five
reference-session support, revisions and methodology boundaries are explicit.
The source download ledger retains PDF metadata, hashes and extracted tables.

Recovered index releases with an official dated announcement and its complete
linked composition can enter at the next decision without a BDI copy. A BDI
can move admission to the announcement day's decision only under the existing
exact-weight and opening-footnote proof. A missing whole effective-composition
cycle cannot cause a much older composition to become the baseline: valid B3
four-month cycles are within 135 calendar days of their previews. This limits
comparisons to the immediately preceding recovered cycle.

The original four balance/utilization lending fields remain unchanged.
The accepted latest-vintage rate archive extends `loan_rate`,
`loan_rate_change_5` and `new_loan_volume_surprise` with the same formulas and
clocks, since that archive includes the corresponding positive registered
quantities. Original observations are preserved, and no zero-flow bulletin
becomes a rate or positive flow observation. All outputs retain the separate
latest-vintage label.
