# Round 5 registered-flow support amendment

This source-coverage amendment changes only `new_loan_volume_surprise`. It is registered before building its replacement feature archive or inspecting any feature outcome. The existing strict archive remains immutable: `lending_feature_archive/manifest.json`, SHA-256 `e5e071bdaadda03eb93bf7b80bc52e0c81ddfb681830e4a8cc7bfee02346100b`, under the registered Round-5 acquisition root.

All 41,353 accepted registered-loan rows have positive, nonmissing share quantity. However, 26 entire source sessions are missing between July and November 2024. Requiring every one of the preceding 20 exchange sessions to be observed yields 23,352 feature observations and none after 2024-07-02. This rejects useful histories even when 15–19 actual observations exist. No source rate or quantity is filled, interpolated or newly admitted by this amendment.

The amended formula is `(current registered quantity - prior mean) / prior sample standard deviation`, using actual observed quantities within the **preceding fixed 20 B3 sessions**, excluding the current session. At least 15 of those 20 sessions must have actual observations; the current quantity must be present and positive; the sample standard deviation must be finite and positive. The window never becomes “the last 20 observed rows”, and fewer than 20 elapsed calendar sessions at the left boundary cannot substitute a shorter window. Missing observations remain missing, not zero. Publication, source age, identity, and every other lending formula remain unchanged.

The coverage-only calculation produces 32,781 valid observations, 9,429 more than the strict contract. It restores 2,540 September, 1,274 October and 832 November 2024 observations while August 2024 remains unavailable because its maximum observed support is 14. These counts use no labels, predictions or returns and are not an outcome-based threshold search. The 15/20 requirement was chosen before this single candidate calculation as a 75% support floor.

Evidence lives at `b3_lending_tables/registered_flow_support_audit.json` and `accepted_rate_calendar_gaps.json` in the registered acquisition root. The accepted original balance and rate rows remain exact. A fresh replacement lending archive will record this amendment, the source-code commit, all source hashes, and the strict-versus-amended coverage comparison. This amendment does not admit the separately archived latest-vintage late-2024 rate candidate or change economic replay costs.
