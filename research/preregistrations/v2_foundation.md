## Evaluation and decision rules (frozen before new results)

Primary forecasting metric: equal-date D3/D5/D10 common-population IC. Primary
economics: the established neutral, cash-permitting allocator, with its fixed
prior-only equal-rank calibration. Use actual inventory and costs, CDI excess net,
utility, turnover, exposure, drawdown, win/loss fractions and three consistent
Sharpes (BRL/CDI, USD/US cash and zero-rate). Do not switch selectors after results.
Report fold and seed dispersion, coverage eras, and paired 20/40/60-session block
intervals respecting fold boundaries; 40 is primary. Shared seed pairs and dates
are dependent. Intervals are nominal, not adjusted for this whole research history.

Screen admission for an improvement: ensemble IC delta >= .001 OR net delta >=
.25 bps/day; both IC delta > -.001 and net delta > -.25; at least two of three
seeds and three of four fold means improve the claimed endpoint. A smaller roster
or model can instead advance for practical noninferiority only if the lower ends
of its paired 90% intervals exceed -.001 IC AND -.25 net bps/day. A wide interval
crossing zero is not equivalence. These are candidate gates, not promotion.

Confirmation: paired 95% lower bound > 0 for the claimed improvement, with 90%
lower bounds above the same noninferiority margins for the other endpoint; or both
90% bounds above the noninferiority margins for a simplification. Require at least
two of three seeds to meet the point-estimate margins. Report all confirmations.
At each wave prefer the simpler admitted candidate, breaking genuine ties by fixed
cell order. If none admits, retain its control. At most one synthesis candidate
enters final confirmation, alongside any separately admitted averaging rule.
No candidate is described as deployment-ready while material accounting assumptions
remain unresolved. A negative result closes that bounded branch, not all alpha research.
