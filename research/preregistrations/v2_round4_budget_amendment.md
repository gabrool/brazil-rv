## Decision and stopping rule

The headline remains the original full three-seed ensemble. A research designation
is seed-stable only if the same non-null provisional designation occurs in the full
panel and all three leave-one-out panels. A working-parent choice is seed-stable
only if the same provisional parent occurs in all four panels and is economically
eligible in each. If the parent choice is unstable, keep fast_off as the research
comparator, label the choice inconclusive, and do not imply that it is deployable.
If the designation is unstable, report no accepted designation. These decisions are
separate: a stable working parent need not imply a stable winning arm.

Stop after these fixed diagnostics and reporting. Do not add seeds until a desired
answer appears. Any further training belongs to a separately registered question.
This closes Round 4 as a three-seed **development** result, never as six-seed
confirmation or independent replication. The overlapping leave-one-out panels are
sensitivity checks, not independent samples, equivalence tests, or new confidence
levels. Temporal confidence intervals remain conditional on the fitted ensemble.
Less direct evidence about unseen random initializations is the explicit tradeoff.
