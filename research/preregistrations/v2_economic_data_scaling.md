# Economic accounting, deep data audit and capacity follow-up

Authorized by the user on 2026-09-19 after the completed foundation program.
This is a new program. Foundation outcomes and failed advancement decisions remain
unchanged; neither wider attention nor GRU is retrospectively declared a winner.
Reread this document after each stage. Progress and evidence belong in
`docs/v2_economic_data_scaling_progress.md` and the corresponding run pointer.

## Fixed boundaries and priorities

Use the local RTX 2060 only. No forward capture, deployment or 2025/2026 consumer
reads. Keep all eligible securities and the full 60-session history. The user
authorizes additional historical B3/issuer/CVM retrieval and evidenced derived-data
repairs. Preserve immutable raw sources, accepted stores and completed fit roots.
Resolve canonical pointers and bind hashes at each run, rather than borrowing a
timestamped directory from memory. New accounting and data contracts require new
artifacts; never silently rescore an old checkpoint on altered input coordinates.

The intended account is initially retail-adjacent; assume zero brokerage as the
user requested. Stock-borrow rent and B3 fees are separate. Broker-specific cash
remuneration, collateral and borrowing commissions cannot be established from
permission alone: distinguish user assumptions, verified historical exchange terms
and unresolved scenarios. Do not invent fees or apply a current tariff to all years.
Use normalized results where scale invariant; if historical fixed minimum charges
require capital, report explicitly assumed R$1m with R$100k/R$10m sensitivities.
These are research sizes, not a capacity or actual-account claim.

## A. Accounting and borrow repair

Trace the exact independent and differentiable account contracts. Establish dated
cash/share obligations, effective/ex/credit/payment dates, signed short obligations,
multi-leg distributions, existing-successor inventory, pending orders and terminal
claims. Replace evidenced missing-quote dispositions with contractual accounting.
An accounting outcome learned later must not enter an earlier trading decision or
valuation using information not yet available. Preserve unresolved claims explicitly;
never turn an absence of quotes into an executable sale.

Start with the source-bound CIEL/BRML/DMMO/CPLE/ALLOS/ISA cases, then rank remaining
events by actual held exposure, not by favorable effect on performance. Cross-check
all related known identity/return discontinuities. Keep announcement-time information,
effective economic succession and custody delivery as distinct concepts.

Audit lending source parsing, rates/units, publication timing, weighted aggregation,
availability proxies, exceptional/revised rows, missing-rate fallback, stock and
hedge costs. Verify contract rate/reference-price/accrual conventions, dated B3 fees,
corporate actions on loans, recall/return and borrowing capacity assumptions. Obtain
additional historical files where useful. Published average rates/loan balances are
proxies, not proof of an executable locate. Quantify unsupported assumptions instead
of either declaring them exact or deleting all uncertain names.

Test conservation of NAV, long/short symmetry where contractual, splits, mixed cash
and shares, multiple successors, merging/netting holdings, delayed payments/delivery,
date boundaries and unaffected-path agreement. Update independent and training
accounts coherently where applicable; leave no silently incompatible active path.

## B. Deep source-to-model data audit

Revisit the actual sources and builders, not just finite-value/coverage summaries.
Cover identity/universe/warm-up; raw prices/volumes and corporate actions; shareholder
wealth and label endpoints/neutralization; every accepted auxiliary family; availability
and revisions; unit/scale and financial denominators; missingness versus known age;
conditioning/scaler inheritance; tensor alignment, dtype, masks and inference reuse.

For each family record source semantics, source-to-store reconciliation, timestamp
contract, extreme/discontinuity examples and consumer behavior. Use stratified dates,
eras/identities and targeted complete scans of derived arrays; do not indiscriminately
recrawl immutable raw directories. Inspect both accidental suppression/delay and
optimistic leakage. Audit deterministic preprocessing before interpreting alpha metrics.
Investigate identified exceptions against original dated evidence; do not fix errors
by clipping, dropping difficult stocks, future revisions or arbitrary support filters.

Repairs must preserve valid information unless a specific observation is demonstrably
wrong. Attribute changed eligibility, history, feature observations and labels per
reason; legitimate identity corrections need not preserve an erroneous old mask.
Build a new derived store only when an actual correction requires it. Verify unaffected
arrays, future-mutation/causality, label boundaries, fit-only transformations and actual
consumer paths. Keep conditioning hypotheses without a correctness basis separate
from repairs and evaluate them as experiments, not automatic improvements.

## C. Reassess existing leads under corrected economics

Replay matched full-input attention, wider attention, GRU with early peers and C6
using existing forecasts where their contracts remain valid. Reproduce unaffected
books before attributing changes. Separate an accounting-only change from new data,
targets, features, training or policy. Refit matched controls when a data repair makes
old fits incomparable. Keep neutral allocation primary; report earlier flexible-net
references separately, not as interchangeable headline results.

The two named foundation leads receive a new economic-primary replication on the
other ten development folds if their corrected four-fold mean net advantage remains
positive and no technical/data failure invalidates the hypothesis. This admission
is explicitly motivated by already observed foundation results. It is not a claim
of an untouched experiment or retroactive passage of the old IC gate. No other
model or loss changes enter that comparison. Reuse compatible parents only.

Primary endpoint: matched net return above CDI; IC is a diagnostic, not a hard veto.
Report BRL/CDI, USD/US-cash and zero-rate Sharpes, utility, turnover, gross/net/beta,
drawdown, win/loss, fees/financing, seed/fold dispersion and actual holding duration
where available. Use paired within-fold 20/40/60-session blocks (40 primary).
For these two nominated replications require net improvement >= .25 bps/day, at
least two of three positive seed deltas, and a one-sided 97.5% lower block bound
above zero (two-candidate family adjustment). A fall in BRL/CDI Sharpe or worse
drawdown prevents automatic adoption and requires explicit risk attribution.
Report nominal reused-development limits and financing-scenario sensitivity.

## D. Bounded scaling experiments

Do not infer a GRU scaling law from a GRU-versus-attention encoder change. Each
architecture gets its own one-axis contrasts once A-C establish valid comparisons.
Retain the full cleaned input package, FiLM, early peers, history pooling, heads,
optimizer recipe and data unless the named contrast changes them.

Priority contrasts: temporal attention width 96 to 128; GRU width 64 to 96. Then
one temporal-depth increase for the retained attention width and one recurrent-depth
increase for the retained GRU width. Freeze actual supported layer settings and
parameter counts before each wave. A matched LSTM versus GRU contrast is authorized
after those recurrent diagnostics; parameter count and runtime differences must
be explicit. Distinguish wider/deeper temporal encoding from extra peer/final-context
capacity. A further peer/context or general-capacity increase is conditional on
module/fit diagnostics and the earlier economic results, not an automatic grid.

At most two candidate cells per GPU wave, four screen folds and seeds 11/29/47,
plus necessary matched controls and compatible P parents. Screen net >= .25 bps/day,
at least two positive seed deltas and three positive fold deltas, without a decline
in Sharpe or worse drawdown unexplained by exposure. IC remains diagnostic. Confirm
at most one retained candidate per architecture on the other ten folds under the
same two-candidate economic replication rule. Do not keep escalating width because
one noisy mean is positive. Record every attempted/skipped branch and reason.

Keep existing selection-aware optimizer/epoch contracts for attribution; economic
primary evaluation does not silently change the training loss or checkpoint selector.
Use compile, FP16 with scaled gradients, full cross-sections and shared immutable
caches where measured beneficial. Benchmark representative full-population steps,
memory and complete fits; do not shorten history, drop names or truncate learning
to make a larger graph look fast. Preserve informative diagnostics without a new
orchestration framework.
