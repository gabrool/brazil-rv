**Brazil-RV: proposed intraday intervention design and preparation for the next research phase**

Prepared 2026-09-09 against repository commit `b8484269495b71601e0d0123816ff51e83f63adf`. This is a proposal for critical review, not an accepted registration or an instruction to run experiments. It does not authorize a holdout read. The questions are how a multiday model should react during the day, and which remaining work should precede a broader improvement campaign.

My recommendation is to retain a daily return forecast, introduce a separately testable intraday risk policy, and let an execution policy implement their resulting orders. Intraday return forecasting can be tested later as a distinct hypothesis. The existing research pipeline is sufficiently mature for controlled experiments; it needs a bounded preparation phase, not another general rewrite or a production trading platform.

**1. The fast-off model still receives the repaired intraday scalar features. Round 1' separately tested intraday scalars in GBDT, which does not establish their contribution to the neural model. Neither experiment evaluated an intraday risk intervention or an execution schedule.

**2. Separate the time scale of the forecast from the time scale of decisions.** A forecast for several days does not require waiting several days to manage the position. Market information can change the risk, cost or feasibility of holding a position even when it does not improve the ranking of future returns.

| Component | What it answers | Initial treatment of intraday information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Return forecast | Which securities offer attractive relative multiday returns? | Keep the accepted daily forecast while testing a genuine slow-only alternative |
| Portfolio risk policy | Is the current exposure still within the chosen risk budget? | Update marked exposures and inspect large market or security shocks; allow bounded risk reductions |
| Execution policy | How and when should an authorized position change be traded? | Use observable prices, trading activity and venue state to control timing, size and fill assumptions |
| Optional future forecast update | Does new information change expected remaining return? | Require a separate chronological predictive experiment before allowing it to rerank or reverse positions |

This separation is consistent with the portfolio-construction literature, which treats forecasts, risk, trading costs and holding costs as distinct inputs. It does not require implementing a full optimizer. My specific proposal below is an engineering choice for this repository, not a universally optimal trading rule. [Boyd et al., Multi-Period Trading via Convex Optimization](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/cvx_portfolio.html).

**3. Start with a small, explicit intraday risk policy.** Use scheduled checks on completed five-minute observations as an initial research clock, subject to verifying archive coverage. Five minutes is a proposed operational convention, not an optimized interval. Start with two mechanisms rather than a collection of technical indicators:

- Recompute actual position weights and market-beta exposure using current observable marks and the existing causal daily economic betas. Rebalance the hedge or reduce exposure when a predeclared risk band is breached. Do not estimate hundreds of new betas or a full covariance matrix from a few intraday observations.
- Test an exceptional-shock intervention: restrict additional exposure and, if the predefined risk condition warrants it, reduce current exposure. Distinguish a broad market move from an unusually large security-specific move. A market decline alone is not a reason to convert a hedged relative-value book into a directional short position.

For a security-specific diagnostic, a simple starting quantity is its return since the relevant reference minus its lagged economic beta times the benchmark return over exactly the same interval. Compare this residual with a causal scale estimated from prior sessions at that clock time. This is a proposed risk diagnostic, not a new baseline alpha feature. For a portfolio-level trigger, use the signed book and hedge exposures; raw index volatility alone can misrepresent the risk of a hedged book. Calibrate any shock threshold on fitting/selection history and freeze it before evaluation.

The first intervention should have limited authority: cancel risk-increasing intentions, reduce an existing equity position without reversing its sign, and adjust the permitted hedge. A trim must still satisfy portfolio constraints: reducing only longs can create unwanted net-short exposure. Do not use a risk trigger to select replacement alpha names or opportunistically increase gross. Ordinary quote updates should not repeatedly resize the whole book.

Define recovery as carefully as intervention. My starting preference is distinct entry/recovery bands, a minimum intervention duration, and equity re-entry at the next regular daily decision. This avoids repeated exit/re-entry around one threshold. Those choices have a cost and must be measured; they are not free safety improvements. A hedge may need its own recovery rule because freezing it can itself create exposure.

The daily alpha clock must be exact. Before today's 15:45 forecast exists, an intraday check can use the previously available daily forecast and actual holdings, not today's eventual score. At and after 15:45 it may use the new forecast only once computation would have completed. Any earlier daily forecast for a future slow-only model is a separate registered clock change.

This policy can respond after a shock becomes observable. It cannot prevent the first price jump, trade through a halt, or eliminate overnight gap losses. A risk exit is an instruction, not a guaranteed fill. B3's documented March 2020 circuit-breaker episode illustrates actual periods when trading was interrupted; the applicable historical rules and auction states must be used in replay. Stop prices also do not guarantee execution prices, while price limits can leave orders unfilled. [B3 historical circuit-breaker notice](https://www.b3.com.br/en_us/news/circuit-breaker-8AE490CA70CB10030170CEECFCE05EAF.htm), [SEC explanation of stop-order mechanics](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-15). The latter explains mechanics, not B3-specific order availability.

**4. Intraday execution is useful, but it cannot replace position-risk decisions.** Slowing or cancelling an entry can reduce implementation cost; it cannot by itself manage the risk of an existing holding. Keep the two purposes identifiable in the ledger.

The current implementation explicitly uses a daily `close_proxy` order model and fixed transaction costs. It is not an intraday simulator. Build a bounded replay path for the new question using the existing signed-share accounting where possible. Freeze the ordering of observations, decisions, latency, orders and fills. A condition measured on a completed bar can only affect a subsequent permitted fill; it cannot earn execution at the same bar's earlier open, at an exact stop threshold, or at a conveniently chosen high/low.

M1 OHLCV is sufficient for some coarse price-path and activity experiments, but it does not identify the order book, bid-ask spread, queue position, or the precise volume available at a particular price. Label the resulting fills as proxies and provide latency, slippage and partial-fill sensitivity. A bar's volume may inform a retrospective fill-capacity bound; it is not known to the decision before that bar occurs. An ex-ante participation plan must use prior information. Do not use MT5 historical `spread` as an executable market spread.

An especially important implementation detail is that the current D1/D2 defect signatures assume the existing fill contract. An intentional participation-limited partial fill must not be labelled a defect merely because the old close proxy expected completion. Likewise, a deliberate risk reduction may take gross below the historical 1.5 minimum or change quintile occupancy. Register meaningful overlay-specific expectations before running it, preserve the original baseline checks, and continue to enforce accounting, chronology and true risk limits. Do not discover this conflict mid-experiment and waive the checks after seeing results.

**5. Use a different intraday representation for risk than for alpha.** Cross-sectional rank-Gaussianized features describe relative ordering and discard the cross-section's absolute scale. If nearly every stock experiences a large shock together, similar ranks do not imply similar absolute risk. The risk policy should therefore consume causal, economically interpretable returns, exposure, activity and scale estimates, outside the neural feature normalization.

Use the current observation masks and dated security identities. Corporate actions and source-unit mismatches must not create false crash triggers; use only adjustments or event information actually available at the time. Do not treat a missing bar as a zero return, manufacture volume, or grant execution at a stale mark. These are research-data semantics, not a proposal for additional file-age checks or production monitoring machinery.

Coverage is a real constraint. The accepted M1 archive covers 158 identities, while the multiday universe is broader; the same-clock relative-volume feature remains almost entirely unavailable after the existing support requirements. We cannot assume complete intraday price, liquidity or hedge coverage from the daily store's completeness. First inventory coverage by date and by the original book's actual held exposure, including short positions and the intended hedge. Sources: [intraday coverage](v2_INTRADAY_COVERAGE.md), [rebuilt-store coverage](v2_REBUILT_STORE.md).

For an initial bounded test, use the same covered evaluation population and fill model for both the intervention and its no-intervention comparator. Also report the fraction of the full book that could not be monitored. Do not claim whole-book protection, retrospectively restrict membership to today's M1 survivors, or silently drop uncovered positions. Reliable broader intraday data is a prerequisite for broader protection claims, not for every daily-alpha experiment.

**6. Allow intraday information to change forecasts only after a separate test.** The clean future hypothesis is whether information arriving after the last daily forecast predicts the remaining return, conditional on that forecast. This could use a small conditional model or a time-conditioned version of the daily model; neither is required for the first risk experiment.

The training examples, clock features, labels and execution endpoints must correspond to each actual decision time. Reusing a model trained on 15:45 snapshots at 11:00 is not automatically valid. Nor can a rank score be treated as a price target and have the day's realized return subtracted from it: the score has no calibrated return units. A news-driven override additionally requires point-in-time event timestamps and historical coverage; price-based risk controls should not be described as understanding the news.

Judge a forecast update on incremental predictive and net economic value after extra turnover. Judge a risk intervention on its risk/cost trade-off. The latter can be useful with unchanged IC and lower mean return if it achieves a previously specified risk objective. Neither label exempts a selected policy from out-of-sample testing.

**7. What should be completed before a broad improvement campaign?** The previous economic-beta, hedge-timing and action-timing repairs are implemented; the replays, rebuilt-store acceptance and Round 3 passed. This review has not established another critical accounting defect. The remaining priorities are explicit contracts and decision-useful measurement.

| Priority | Recommended work | Definition of done / when it is needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before the next candidate comparison | Register the forecast/portfolio objective and selection rule | State the return type, decision/fill times, main horizon metric, risk constraints, permitted cost of risk reduction, candidate budget and promotion rule before results |
| Before claiming implementable net improvement | Specify economic assumptions at meaningful capital | Use an explicit capital/ticket-size range; report capacity, delayed/partial fills, transaction costs, borrow availability/rates, hedge costs and financing sensitivities |
| Before interpreting intraday intervention | Audit covered exposures and implement the minimal chronological replay | Same-accounting no-intervention comparator, causal clock tests, no fabricated fills, explicit gaps/halts/action handling and intervention recovery |
| Before spending the official 2025 read | Freeze what the read will decide | One candidate/policy, predeclared decision rule, no tuning on its outcome; preserve the existing disclosure about historical integrity-only scans |
| At the start of model improvement work | Run a genuinely slow-only neural ablation | Remove current intraday scalars and their masks/ages as inputs, native fast and collection-presence information; compare with matched seeds, chronology, targets and policy |
| Before live deployment, rather than every research run | Upgrade execution and event evidence to the intended trading setup | Contractual corporate actions and dated calendar/auction facts, tradable price/volume evidence, realistic locates and financing, plus prospective operational validation |

The first row includes a current mismatch worth resolving deliberately. The neural loss averages D1/D2/D3/D5/D10, primary model selection uses D1/D2/D3/D5, and the adopted execution composite uses D3/D5/D10. That is not proof of a bug: auxiliary horizons can help representation learning. But we should explicitly register the primary metric for the intended holding horizon and retain the historical metric as a secondary readout. Any change to head weighting or the learned target is then a paired experiment, not a cleanup. Sources: [loss implementation](../research/src/brazil_rv/v2/losses.py), [horizon contract](../research/src/brazil_rv/v2/contract.py), [selected execution policy](v2_R31_EXECUTION_SWEEP.md).

Economic calibration should start with attribution rather than another optimization sweep. The existing report already contains cost scenarios, borrow cases, sterile proceeds and accounting diagnostics; use them. Current assumptions include fixed 4 bps per side, a close-price proxy, full short-proceeds remuneration and zero debit spread by default. Published lending balances/rates are not proof of a historical locate. A liquidity threshold such as R$20m of prior daily volume does not establish that our intended order fills at the headline price. The relevant question is how much of the measured advantage survives plausible implementation assumptions at the intended scale. The direction of the bias can differ by assumption. Source: [current ledger configuration](../research/src/brazil_rv/execution/stateful_ledger.py).

Keep calendar reconstruction, inferred corporate-action terms and terminal-settlement conventions visible. Audit event-level P&L concentration and settlement contributions before crediting an apparent improvement. Correct a demonstrated material error before dependent experiments; do not require a complete institutional data replacement merely to compare two otherwise identical daily predictors. The historical 2025 integrity-only payload scans have already been disclosed; they were not performance evaluations, and their disclosure must remain. The previously spent test is not a new holdout. Source: [current project context](../PROJECT_CONTEXT.md).

**8. The first research experiments should be small and distinguishable.** These are proposals requiring a registration, not fixes to execute automatically:

1. Compare a genuine slow-only neural model against the existing scalar-only fast-off model on matched seeds and folds. If constructing the slow-only model changes its pretraining graph, train appropriate fresh P checkpoints and record that full contract; do not force incompatible checkpoints into it. Preserve B6 as the historical accepted reference. Three matched seeds can screen new hypotheses if registered in advance; use the predeclared confirmation seed set for promotion.
2. With forecasts frozen, test no intraday intervention versus exposure/hedge-band control, then the same control plus one exceptional-shock rule. Do not combine forecast, sizing, execution and risk changes in one initial test. This risk work can proceed independently of daily feature experiments once its own data/replay prerequisites are met.
3. Test horizon alignment and cost-aware portfolio construction. Start by making the existing head/book relationship explicit; later consider borrowing costs and a calibrated trade-versus-hold decision. Equal slots and volatility quintiles may restrict alpha, but removing them changes exposures and must be evaluated with matched risk constraints.
4. Add information only through causal, separate ablations. The rebuilt lending and oddlot families exist but were not enabled in Round-3 neural inputs. Their data preparation need not be repeated without a discovered defect. Common-state scale information is another distinct candidate because cross-sectional ranks omit it.
5. Then test retained intraday-era training choices in a bounded way: transfer scope, learning-rate treatment of newly useful branches, SAM and stopping behavior. The current P-to-F handoff transfers all compatible non-fast-encoder parameters; the recorded gates and gradients do not show a dead fast branch, so increasing gate initialization is not a demonstrated repair. Do not mix these changes with the first feature comparison. Source: [P-to-F transfer](../research/src/brazil_rv/v2/train.py), [recorded branch diagnostics](v2_ROUND3.md).

Do not remove the accepted v1 intraday program or immutable raw M1 data because a v2 ablation failed. After a new v2 default is accepted, remove only genuinely superseded v2 paths, unused configuration and dead tests; keep the measured comparison reproducible through Git and sealed artifacts. Avoid a new execution framework, feature registry, reinforcement-learning agent, or broad hyperparameter search as preparation.

**9. Evaluate the intervention against the question it is meant to answer.** Freeze triggers, thresholds, re-entry rules, data coverage and fill semantics before evaluating. Report paired after-cost return alongside intraday drawdown, tail loss, largest adverse excursions, time outside risk limits, turnover, missed recoveries, fill delays and exposure. Explicitly identify each intervention's reason and subsequent outcome.

Include a simpler static lower-exposure comparator with its setting chosen before evaluation. Otherwise, an overlay can appear to improve risk merely by holding less of everything. Do not normalize using future realized volatility and then call the comparison tradable. A lower-exposure comparator does not replace the unchanged full-exposure baseline; both answer useful questions.

Use chronological development folds and paired block uncertainty, with interventions from one market event treated as clustered rather than independent trades. Rare crises may leave too few events for precise tail claims. A March 2020 stress exercise is not an out-of-sample model backtest if its scores came from a model trained through or after that period; it needs an appropriately earlier fit, or an explicit label as a mechanical/in-sample stress exercise.

My recommendation is to keep the 2025 performance read unspent through this bounded preparation and the newly registered initial comparison, then decide on the frozen candidate under a written protocol. Meeting the existing development criteria is encouraging; it is neither an instruction to reuse 2025 repeatedly nor a reason to keep changing the gate until a preferred result emerges. Model/feature experiments and the overlay also remain exposed to development-set selection bias; record unsuccessful trials as well as winners.

**10. Questions for the reviewing LLM.** Evaluate the proposal against the linked repository evidence and primary sources. Distinguish a confirmed defect from an untested modeling choice. In particular:

- Does the evidence justify removing native fast from a future v2 default, retaining only scalar intraday inputs, or testing a true slow-only model first? What does it not establish?
- Is a limited risk-and-execution policy the best first response to intraday shocks, or is there a concrete reason to change return predictions immediately?
- Are the proposed clock, coverage, action, hedge, fill and recovery contracts sufficiently causal? Identify any remaining route to optimistic protection or future leakage.
- Which preparation items block all model research, which block only intraday/economic claims, and which belong to live deployment? Remove unnecessary work and add only material omissions.
- What is the smallest useful experiment sequence, with a predeclared risk/cost success criterion, that could reject this recommendation? Assess the proposed timing of the one-time 2025 read separately.

Return specific amendments with rationale. Do not treat the proposed thresholds, five-minute clock, slow-only candidate or delayed validation read as already approved research decisions.
