## Contract and historical information

Approved renewal remains the primary 63-session hypothesis, requested four sessions
early; 30/126-session bounds remain registered. The new denied endpoint refuses
every due renewal after that day's intentions/fills. It preserves the old principal,
reference, rate, accrued charges and proceeds, and requires a cover at the next
decision. The explicit response closes the whole same-security book, including
younger cohorts, preserving existing proportional physical-return allocation.
It does not identify lender-level locates or pick the cheapest cohort.

Ordinary recalls use explicit notice dates and two/four-session physical-return
deadlines. They arrive before decisions, after known morning custody delivery.
Borrowing is blocked through the stated recall interval and until called contracts
physically return. Afterward, later independent locates are permitted by the existing
availability hypothesis. Notice rights, deadlines and eventual locates are research
assumptions, not broker quotes. A denied loan's deadline is its original maturity.

Missing quotes, partial fills and locked corporate entitlements never manufacture
execution. Loans continue their existing dated rent/fee obligations; overdue principal
is exposed daily and marks economics unresolved. No punitive fee, buy-in price,
replacement loan or proceeds release is invented. Deadlines survive partial-return
splits, corporate delivery and independent copied training state. Pending exits and
hedge loans receive the same constraints. Arbitrary target books use `book_summary`;
the fixed-slot summary contract remains separate.
