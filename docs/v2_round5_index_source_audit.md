# Round 5 index source audit

The historical index family is recoverable. The audit retrieved official release pages, linked composition workbooks and dated B3 BDI02 bulletins for 19 releases from May 2023 through December 2024. Six releases extend the old archive: August 1/16/29 previews, September 2 effective composition and December 2/16 previews. These cover IBOV, IBXX and SMLL with 5,777 published constituent weights. No current constituent list reconstructs historical membership.

Sources, hashes and exact event dates are recorded in `D:\quant-data\b3\interim\round5_data_20260910T140442Z\index_audit\source_events.json`. The original 13-release archive remains immutable. Additional files were retrieved from the official B3 pages, including their explicitly linked PR attachment host; no unlinked workbook is accepted. Full compositions are distinguished from separate entry/exit spreadsheets by the existing complete-portfolio parser.

**What the signal means.** The admitted candidate is explicitly
`preview_minus_last_effective_weight_proxy_contains_price_drift`. Dated BDI tables labelled current can repeat the last effective weights. For example, December 2023 current weights retain the September effective portfolio, while the preview weights are newly published. Their current-date footnote does not turn these into drift-adjusted current weights. Reconstructing genuine weights would require verified quantities, corporate actions and action-safe prices for every constituent; that reconstruction is not claimed.

For an active security, pressure sums the three indexes' published preview minus last-effective weight, in percentage points, multiplies by remaining B3 sessions to the announced effective date, and divides by its prior 20-session cash ADV in BRL millions. The fixed scaling is a unit conversion, not an estimate of fund assets. Price drift contaminates this proxy. There is no pure flow claim, assumed index-fund AUM, technical post-effective reversal, or synthetic current weight.

**Publication evidence.** Official announcement pages expose historical publication dates. HTTP Last-Modified is not a publication clock. Twelve release events have complete exact matching BDI weights and opening-table footnotes; they are usable at that day's 15:45 decision. Seven events use a date-only end-of-day availability bound and the next decision: August 1/16/31 and September 4, 2023, plus August 16/29 and September 2, 2024. The August 2023/September 2023 and September 2024 BDI PDFs omit the relevant tables. August 16/29, 2024 match the complete workbook except that the BDI text lacks GGPS3 in IBXX and BRKM5 in SMLL; a full same-day vector is not claimed from that incomplete corroboration. The normalized manifest lists differences per event instead of hiding them.

Effective portfolios serve as historical reference weights only after their disclosure. Each later preview replaces the prior preview at its own first eligible decision. Pressure stops before the effective session opens. A missing prior portfolio or unresolved constituent identity is not treated as zero information. Nonmembership in complete known portfolios has an observed zero weight. `index_event_age` counts sessions since the current preview first became decision-available; both features also retain explicit source-age provenance.

**Identity and the single first-day case.** Snapshot tickers join to the latest dated COTAHIST ISIN assignment at or before the snapshot date, with no later identity row. Renames sharing an ISIN combine across snapshots. The audit found one assignment first appearing on its publication day: AZZA3 on August 1, 2024. [B3 circular 012/2024-VNC](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/FE/11/55/95/09BE09105FE89209AC094EA8/OC%20012-2024-VNC%20Tratamento%20Carteiras%20de%20%C3%8Dndices%20da%20B3%20-%20Evento%20de%20Incorpora%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20do%20Grupo%20de%20Moda%20Soma%20pela%20Arezzo_PT.pdf), dated July 25, explicitly announces the August 1 ticker change and index treatment. It proves the ticker was known before opening; day-dated COTAHIST supplies the ISIN identifier. The circular does not spell out the new ISIN, and no same-day COTAHIST price is used. The source catalog binds this exception; another first-day identity without prior announcement fails the build.

**Calendar boundary.** December 2024 previews announce a January 6, 2025 effective date. [B3 circular 149/2024-PRE](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/58/42/2C/24/0FDF29106EEC8429AC094EA8/OC%20149-2024%20PRE%20Calendario%20de%20Feriados%20em%202025%20e%20Funcionamento%20da%20B3%20em%2005032025%20(Quarta-Feira%20de%20Cinzas)%20(PT).pdf), dated November 5, 2024, establishes the known January calendar. Only January 2/3/6 future session metadata extends the remaining-session count; no 2025 price, outcome or consumer row is read. The B3 news article carrying the calendar was posted December 4, but the underlying circular predates both December previews.

**Implementation and validation.** `round5_index.py` reuses the canonical complete-workbook parser and prior-ADV20 calculation. It opens the registered development store with valid sample dates plus causal history, respecting the July 2016 embargo. Eight targeted tests protect separate BDI phases, Brazilian numeric units, explicit source links, date-only availability, dated renames, future mutation, preview replacement, effective-session cutoff, missing-source masks and explicit future calendars. The build additionally runs actual joined-output future mutation and first-decision mutation checks. Normalized and feature manifests bind a clean code commit, source hashes and the final data hash.

**Forward capture.** The official current SPA exposes configuration, current-day composition, theoretical composition and quarterly preview endpoints. Configuration is essential: the preview endpoint can return an old portfolio even when its `has` flag is zero. Forward snapshots must preserve all raw responses, UTC first-capture times, pagination completeness and the active-preview flag. They remain quarantined from development; a current API response is not a historical publication vintage.
