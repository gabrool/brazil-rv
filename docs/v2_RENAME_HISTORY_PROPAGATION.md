The three previously admitted ALLOS/ISA links now propagate through the full
3,717-session, 933-name daily axes, with the 2009 source warmup retained. This
is an intermediate derived contract; the accepted store and old fits stay sealed.
Resolve `rename_history_propagation`, `rename_peer_propagation` and
`rename_input_audit` in `v2_economic_data_scaling_run.json`.

The source-bound changes recover 60 ALOS3 and 28 ISAE4 eligible sessions and
retire twelve stale predecessor entries. ISAE3 retains its history but does not
pass the existing liquidity rule. All names remain on the permanent axis. Raw
quotes are unchanged. The q=1/cash=0 conversions add 461 valid name/horizon
outcomes without losing any existing valid outcome. Earlier labels remain exact
outside the ten **equity-session** windows that can cross an identity boundary.
Characteristic-neutral targets remain the existing virtual consumer calculation;
their changed feature/return inputs require the new store/refit contract.

All 32 slow fields, risk scales, common state and gross shareholder/price labels
were rebuilt. The first implementation recovered wealth history but exposed two
consumer gaps: the neural window still read the successor's empty older column,
and monthly clustering still counted only its short post-listing record. Both
are repaired. Unit/no-cash history mappings bind effect and announcement clocks.
A later decision reads predecessor values, masks and ages for the historical
portion of its 60-session window while retaining the successor's permanent output
index. The public successor coordinate remains empty before birth; internal
continuation history used during feature construction is kept separately.

Monthly clusters retain an existing assignment through a rename. At the next
monthly fit they read the predecessor's preceding observations under the same
126-session/101-observation rules. The first post-rename scalar snapshot also
inherits the predecessor's previous-session peer values. No peer threshold changes.
The cluster correction alone adds 1,465 valid fields versus the first rename
amendment. The combined change adds 5,453 valid slow-field observations and removes
214 versus the sealed store: 124 at retired predecessor entries and 90 at still
eligible entries whose recomputed cluster has only two other valid peers instead
of the required three. Every one of those 90 cases is enumerated and checked in
`rename_peer_history/field_deltas.json`; no security or source observation is dropped.
There are 642,975 changed shared valid slow values, principally from cross-sectional
ranks, corrected history and recomputed peers. The other 27 fields and pre-event
prefix remain exact during the narrow five-field cluster repair.

Twelve actual consumer dates cover before/at/after the renames and later monthly
updates. All 933 names, full 60-session windows, masks, ages, permanent indices and
packed CPU tensors reconcile independently: 65,171,916 cells, zero mismatches.
The last pre-ALLOS-rename tensor equals the original store exactly. Separate tests
cover late announcement clocks, compact names, authorization, actual store building,
cluster carry, monthly historical support and future-data causality. No neural
